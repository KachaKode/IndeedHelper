Title: Palantir Developer (Subcontract at Ark Technology Consultants)

Through Ark Technology Consultants (Ark) and Strategic Resource Services (SRS), we offer custom talent solutions to help our clients meet their evolving technology and business needs. We help effectively match the right technology professional to their organization, recruiting for contract, contract-to-hire, and direct roles. Please note that this is a (contract/contract to hire/direct hire) opportunity with our client and NOT with Ark.

Palantir Developer - Financial Services

Position Summary

We are seeking a Palantir Developer to build and support data-driven applications on the Palantir Foundry and AIP platforms. This role will work closely with business and technology teams to deliver solutions that enhance risk management, regulatory compliance, operations, and financial analytics.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain applications within Palantir Foundry and AIP.
  • Build and optimize data pipelines and integrations from enterprise systems.
  • Design Ontology objects, workflows, and business logic.
  • Create dashboards, operational applications, and user-facing solutions.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to translate business requirements into technical solutions.
  • Support data governance, security, and compliance standards.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve application and data quality issues.

Required Qualifications

  • 3+ years of software development or data engineering experience.
  • Hands-on experience with Palantir Foundry.
  • Strong SQL and Python skills.
  • Experience with APIs, data modeling, and ETL processes.
  • Ability to work directly with business stakeholders.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with Palantir AIP, Ontology, Workshop, and OSDK.
  • Experience with React, TypeScript, or JavaScript.
  • Financial Services industry experience.
  • Exposure to AWS, Azure, or GCP.

Preferred Domain Experience

  • Risk Management
  • Regulatory Reporting
  • AML / KYC
  • Fraud Detection
  • Commercial or Investment Banking
  • Wealth Management

Technologies

Palantir Foundry, AIP, Ontology, Workshop, OSDK, Python, SQL, REST APIs, React, TypeScript, Azure, AWS

Ideal Candidate: A hands-on developer who can leverage Palantir’s platform to transform complex financial data into scalable applications and actionable business insights.

Pay: $80.00 - $120.00 per hour

Expected hours: 40.0 per week

Work Location: Remote
