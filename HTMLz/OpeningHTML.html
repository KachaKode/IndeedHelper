About the Firm

Dark Alpha Capital is an independent sponsor investment firm focused on acquiring and investing in lower middle market businesses across a range of industries. We partner with business owners, management teams, family offices, financial institutions, and institutional investors to identify, evaluate, and execute private market investment opportunities that create long-term value.

Our approach combines disciplined investment processes, operational excellence, technology-driven execution, and strategic partnerships. We leverage proprietary systems, artificial intelligence, automation, and data infrastructure to improve sourcing, investment evaluation, portfolio operations, and firm-wide decision-making.

As a growing investment platform, technology is a critical component of our ability to scale. Our Technology Department works closely with every function of the firm, including Origination, Deal Team, Capital Markets, Operations, Legal, Recruiting, and Finance, to design and implement systems that improve efficiency, intelligence, and execution.

We value ownership, intellectual curiosity, accountability, and a bias toward execution. Team members are given meaningful responsibility, direct exposure to strategic initiatives, and the opportunity to contribute to building the infrastructure that supports a rapidly growing investment firm.

Role Summary

The Technical Project Manager (VP Level) is a senior technology execution leader responsible for managing and delivering firm-wide technology initiatives across all departments within Dark Alpha Capital.

This role serves as the bridge between business stakeholders, senior management, and the Technology Department by translating business requirements into technical projects, managing execution timelines, and ensuring successful delivery of technology solutions that create measurable business value.

The Technical Project Manager owns the end-to-end lifecycle of technology initiatives, including project intake, requirements gathering, prioritization, roadmap development, execution oversight, stakeholder communication, and operational adoption.

This position operates similarly to a Chief of Staff for technology initiatives, ensuring that technology projects remain aligned with firm strategy, departmental objectives, and operational priorities.

The ideal candidate combines strong project management capabilities with technical fluency, allowing them to understand software architecture, automation workflows, APIs, databases, cloud infrastructure, and AI-enabled systems while effectively coordinating engineering resources.

  • Own the firm-wide technology project portfolio across all departments, including investment, capital markets, operations, legal, recruiting, and business development functions.
  • Manage the complete lifecycle of technology initiatives from initial request through implementation and adoption.
  • Establish project priorities, timelines, milestones, dependencies, and success criteria.
  • Maintain visibility into project status, risks, resource requirements, and delivery expectations.
  • Ensure technology initiatives align with broader firm objectives and operational goals.

  • Partner with department heads and senior leadership to identify operational challenges and technology opportunities.
  • Translate business requirements into clear technical specifications, workflows, and implementation plans.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Apply fundamental finance and business principles when evaluating technology initiatives and resource allocation.

Process Improvement & Automation

  • Identify opportunities to automate repetitive workflows and improve operational efficiency.
  • Support development of AI-powered systems, internal platforms, dashboards, and data infrastructure.
  • Evaluate technology solutions through an operational improvement and value creation lens.
  • Develop scalable processes that improve firm-wide productivity.

Leadership & Team Development

  • Establish project management standards, documentation practices, and execution frameworks.

  • Act as a strategic technology leader supporting senior management initiatives.
