Job Overview

We are seeking a dynamic and innovative AI Developer to join our forward-thinking technology team. In this role, you will spearhead the development, implementation, and optimization of cutting-edge artificial intelligence solutions that drive business insights and operational efficiencies. Your expertise will enable us to harness the power of machine learning, natural language processing, and big data analytics to solve complex problems and deliver scalable AI models across diverse platforms. This position offers an exciting opportunity to work with advanced cloud services, big data systems, and state-of-the-art frameworks in a fast-paced environment committed to technological excellence.

Duties

  • Design, develop, and deploy AI models utilizing frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, and other machine learning tools to address real-world challenges.
  • Implement unsupervised learning algorithms for data mining and predictive modeling analysis to uncover hidden patterns and insights.
  • Manage large-scale data pipelines using ETL processes, Talend, Hadoop, Spark, and SQL databases to ensure high-quality data for model training and evaluation.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate AI solutions into existing systems using Java, Python, R, and C for seamless deployment.
  • Optimize AI models for scalability and performance on cloud platforms like AWS Machine Learning Cloud Services while ensuring robust model evaluation and statistical analysis.
  • Develop natural language processing applications for text analytics and conversational AI leveraging generative AI techniques.
  • Conduct statistical modeling and analysis using SAS, R, and statistical analysis tools to support research initiatives.
  • Design database schemas with best practices in database design to support efficient data retrieval for analytics purposes.
  • Utilize big data systems such as Hadoop and Spark implementation to process vast datasets efficiently.
  • Support model deployment strategies ensuring continuous monitoring, evaluation, and improvement of AI models in production environments.

Experience

  • Proven experience in developing AI solutions with a strong background in machine learning frameworks like TensorFlow or PyTorch.
  • Hands-on expertise with cloud-based machine learning services on AWS or similar platforms.
  • Demonstrated ability to work with big data technologies including Hadoop, Spark, and ETL tools such as Talend.
  • Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, Java, R, C, Bash (Unix shell), VBA or similar scripting languages for automation and integration tasks.
  • Experience designing scalable SQL databases and implementing data mining techniques for large datasets.
  • Knowledge of natural language processing (NLP), generative AI models, and predictive modeling analysis is highly desirable.
  • Familiarity with statistical analysis tools like SAS or R for research-driven insights.
  • Understanding of AI implementation strategies including model training, evaluation, deployment, and scalability considerations.
  • Background in data analytics combined with experience in machine learning/AI-based analysis to inform decision-making processes.
  • Exposure to quantum engineering concepts or advanced big data systems is a plus but not required.

Join us as an AI Developer if you’re passionate about pushing the boundaries of artificial intelligence technology! Bring your expertise in machine learning frameworks, cloud services, big data systems, and statistical analysis tools — all aimed at transforming complex data into actionable insights that propel our organization forward!
